# Understanding Rust and Crustify research

# Read this first

A beginner-friendly guide to the research, from memory safety and Rust ownership through unsafe code, FFI, static analysis, formal verification, and the proposed Crustify experiment. No prior Rust or proof-assistant knowledge is assumed.

## Reading paths

- **Learn from scratch:** Chapters 1–5 explain the foundations.
- **Understand the research:** Chapters 6–8 connect those foundations to the project and tools.
- **Prepare for the call:** Chapter 11 gives the short summary and meeting agenda.
- **Plan the work:** Chapters 9–10 describe the proposed experiment and evaluation.
- **Look up a term or source:** use the glossary and linked reading list at the end.

Open the original PDF above for the fully formatted 15-page guide. All pages are also searchable below. The tool choices and experimental plan are proposals, not claims of completed proofs or experiments.
